Digital risk assessment software: a working prototype based on real risk assessments and following the Brazilian Regulatory Standards (NRs).

- Compatible with the databases companies already use.
- Team management: create, update and remove teams.
- Team entry and exit authorization.
- Custom selection of the risks faced.
